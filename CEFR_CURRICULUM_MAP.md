# CEFR CURRICULUM MAP — IELTS BY GAMA

**Curriculum Version:** 2.0 (Phase 12 Expanded)  
**Standard:** Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR A1–C2)  
**Total Lessons:** 54 Structured Lessons | **Modules:** 9 per Level | **Total Estimated Hours:** 42.5 Hours  

---

## 1. Curriculum Overview Matrix

| Level | CEFR Code | Target IELTS Equivalent | Description | Total Modules | Est. Hours |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Beginner** | A1 | Band 2.0–3.0 | Basic vocabulary, concrete everyday phrases, introductions, phonetics | 9 | 5.5 hrs |
| **Elementary** | A2 | Band 3.5–4.0 | Past simple, travel, basic writing, questions, survival English | 9 | 6.0 hrs |
| **Intermediate** | B1 | Band 4.5–5.0 | Present perfect, paragraphing, active listening, opinions | 9 | 7.0 hrs |
| **Upper Intermediate**| B2 | Band 5.5–6.5 | Conditionals, academic lexis, PEEL essays, cohesive devices | 9 | 8.0 hrs |
| **Advanced** | C1 | Band 7.0–8.0 | Inversion, cleft sentences, nuanced hedging, academic synthesis | 9 | 8.0 hrs |
| **Proficiency** | C2 | Band 8.5–9.0 | Subjunctive, idiomatic mastery, spontaneous discourse, stylistic control | 9 | 8.0 hrs |

## 2. A1 Beginner Level Curriculum

### Lesson A1.1 — Grammar: Present Simple & Subject-Verb Concord
- **Lesson ID:** `curric_a1_gram_present_simple`
- **Module:** Grammar
- **Learning Objectives:**
  1. Understand when to use Present Simple for habits and routines.
  2. Master third-person singular inflection (`-s` / `-es`).
  3. Formulate accurate negative sentences using `does not` / `do not`.
