## 1. Official Examination Status & Certification Disclaimer

- **Not an Official Examination**: IELTS by GAMA is an independent, computer-based English learning and practice examination environment. It is **not affiliated with, approved by, or endorsed by** Cambridge Assessment English, the British Council, or IDP: IELTS Australia.
- **Estimated Diagnostic Band Scores**: All band scores generated by the platform (e.g., Band 6.5, Band 7.0) are diagnostic approximations formulated through rule-based psychometric algorithms, NLP heuristics, and objective answer keys. They do **not** constitute official IELTS test results and cannot be submitted for university admissions, professional licensing, or immigration applications.

---

## 2. Audio Asset Availability & Synthesis Reality

- **Unbundled Studio Audio Files**: To maintain an agile, lightweight, offline-first application bundle and to strictly respect copyright boundaries, **0 pre-recorded studio `.mp3` or `.wav` IELTS audio files are distributed within the repository**.
- **Delivery Mechanism**: Listening dialogue and monologue scripts are rendered dynamically via the browser's native **Web Speech API (`window.speechSynthesis`)**.
- **Acoustic Variability**: Voice timbre, cadence, and accent rendering (British, Australian, North American) are contingent on the specific speech synthesis engines and voice packs installed on the host operating system (e.g., Microsoft Natural Voices on Windows, Google TTS on Android, Apple Siri voices on iOS/macOS).

---

## 3. Speaking & Pronunciation Evaluation Modalities

- **Speech-to-Text Transcript Matching**: Automated speech evaluation operates through browser-based Speech-to-Text (STT) transcript generation followed by lexical, syntactic, and coherence analysis.
- **Absence of Acoustic Spectrogram Analysis**: The engine **does not perform** acoustic formant frequency analysis, pitch contour mapping, phonemic duration measurement, or vocal tract resonance modeling.
- **Pronunciation Ratings**: Pronunciation clarity scores reflect the word recognition confidence and transcription accuracy of the underlying speech recognizer. Learners seeking targeted accent reduction or intonation training are advised to compare their recorded speech against native reference audio models and consult qualified human instructors.

---

## 4. Automated Writing Assessment Heuristics

- **Scoring Heuristics**: Automated Writing evaluation analyzes word count boundaries, paragraph architecture, cohesive transitions, academic collocation density, lexical diversity (Type-Token Ratio), and grammatical clause complexity.
- **Confidence Intervals**: Because automated scoring cannot fully interpret philosophical nuance or rhetorical subtlety, all writing band scores include a **calibrated confidence range of $\pm 0.5$ to $1.0$ band score**.
- **Human Examiner Complement**: For high-stakes preparation, learners are encouraged to have periodic essays reviewed by certified human English language instructors.

---

## 5. Client Platform & Hardware Dependencies

- **Microphone Permissions**: The interactive Speaking module requires candidate microphone authorization in modern browsers and Android WebViews.
- **Offline PWA Capabilities**: While all question banks, reading texts, listening scripts, vocabulary vault items, and database engines operate 100% offline, speech recognition requires either local OS speech models or active internet connectivity depending on the browser platform's Web Speech API implementation.
