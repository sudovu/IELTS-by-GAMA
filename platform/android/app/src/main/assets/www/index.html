Question Analysis Questionnaire (The 100% Rule)

Pillar 1: Task Response

Select an official essay prompt or type your own question to deconstruct it into its fundamental components.

Quick Prompts:

STEM vs Creative Arts in High School Curricula

Some educationalists argue that high school curricula should prioritize STEM subjects over creative arts to ensure economic competitiveness. Others believe artistic subjects are equally vital. Discuss both views and give your opinion. (250+ words).

STEP 1

General Topic

Education & Curriculum Design

The broad umbrella category for your vocabulary pool.
STEP 2

Specific Micro-Topic

Whether schools should prioritize STEM over Arts or maintain balance

The exact boundaries. Writing outside this causes a Band 5 Task Response penalty!
STEP 3

Task Words & Instructions

Discuss BOTH views AND give your OWN opinion

Must balance both sides equally and maintain a clear personal stance throughout.

⚠️ Examiner Trap Warning:

Do not spend the entire essay defending only one perspective! Because the prompt specifies "Discuss both views", spending 80% on STEM and 20% on arts automatically limits Task Achievement to Band 6.

The Coffee Shop Method (Natural Idea Generation)

Anti-Panic Strategy

"Imagine you are in a coffee shop with a friend discussing this topic. What would you say in simple, logical terms? Examiners reward clear, realistic arguments—never memorize artificial 'genius' ideas!"

Band 7+ Idea Quality Filter:

  • ✅ Is it easy to explain? If an idea takes 3 complex sentences just to define, drop it.
  • ✅ Can you give a concrete example? Avoid fake statistics ("A Harvard 2019 survey says 99%..."). Use real instances, policies, or documented trends.
  • ✅ Does it directly address the micro-topic? Every point must link directly back to the question prompt.

The PEEL Paragraph Blueprint (Point, Explain, Example, Link)

Band 8+ Coherence

Structure each body paragraph with surgical logic using the IELTS Advantage PEEL blueprint.

P - Point

Topic Sentence: State ONE central controlling idea clearly.

STEM disciplines directly power technological modernization and industrial competitiveness.
E - Explain

The 'Why' / 'How': Explain the underlying mechanism or societal consequence.

Modern knowledge economies require qualified software engineers, data analysts, and researchers to drive productivity.
E - Example

Concrete Evidence: Illustrate with a realistic, recognizable real-world scenario.

For instance, nations that subsidized technology education in secondary schools witnessed marked growth in high-tech exports.
L - Link / Result

Summary & Impact: Conclude the thought and tie back to your overarching position.

Consequently, allocating robust curriculum hours to STEM subjects is economically justified.
