# CEFR PROGRESSION FRAMEWORK — IELTS BY GAMA

**Framework Version:** 3.0 (Phase 13 Comprehensive Depth)  
**Standard:** Council of Europe Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR)  
**Target Audience:** General English Learners (A1–C2) and IELTS Candidates (Academic & General Training)  

---

## 1. Architectural Philosophy of Progression

A genuine language learning progression is not simply a linear list of topics or an arbitrary collection of exercises. It is an incrementally scaffolding cognitive ladder where:
1. **Linguistic Complexity Expands Gradually:** Phonology and simple formulaic expressions (A1) $\rightarrow$ narrative syntax and daily transactions (A2) $\rightarrow$ aspectual contrasts and paragraph cohesion (B1) $\rightarrow$ hypothetical reasoning, academic lexis, and balanced argumentation (B2) $\rightarrow$ syntactic inversion, pragmatic hedging, and evidential critique (C1) $\rightarrow$ stylistic ellipsis, idiomatic nuance, and effortless rhetorical command (C2).
2. **True Qualitative Differences Across Levels:**
   - **A1:** Can understand and use familiar everyday expressions and basic phrases aimed at the satisfaction of needs of a concrete type.
   - **A2:** Can understand sentences and frequently used expressions related to areas of most immediate relevance (basic personal/family info, shopping, geography, employment).
   - **B1:** Can understand the main points of clear standard input on familiar matters regularly encountered in work, school, leisure. Can produce simple connected text on topics of personal interest.
   - **B2:** Can understand the main ideas of complex text on both concrete and abstract topics, including technical discussions in their field of specialization. Can produce clear, detailed text on a wide range of subjects.
   - **C1:** Can understand a wide range of demanding, longer texts, and recognize implicit meaning. Can express ideas fluently and spontaneously without much obvious searching for expressions.
   - **C2:** Can understand with ease virtually everything heard or read. Can summarize information from different spoken and written sources, reconstructing arguments and accounts in a coherent presentation.
3. **No Cross-Level Contamination:** A1 materials do not introduce complex subordination or academic abstractions. C1 and C2 materials do not simply recycle intermediate topics with rare synonyms, but engage with abstract dialectics, epistemic modality, pragmatics, and rhetorical prose.

## 3. Detailed Level Specifications

### Level A1: Beginner (IELTS Equivalent: Band 2.0–3.0)

#### Learning Outcomes
- Introduce oneself and others; ask and answer questions about personal details (residence, family, possessions).
- Read simple notices, signs, departure boards, and short transactional messages.
- Articulate the English alphabet, short and long vowels, and recognize basic IPA symbols.

#### Module Progression
1. **Grammar:** Present Simple & Subject-Verb Concord (`curric_a1_gram_present_simple`)
   - Formulas: Affirmative ($S + V(-s/-es)$), Negative ($S + \text{do/does not} + V_{base}$), Question ($\text{Do/Does} + S + V_{base}?$).
2. **Vocabulary:** Everyday Essentials: Numbers, Family & Time (`curric_a1_vocab_everyday_essentials`)
   - Prepositions of time: `at` (specific hour), `on` (days/dates), `in` (months/years).
3. **Reading:** Notices, Schedules & Short Messages (`curric_a1_read_notices`)
   - Strategy: Locating factual information without translating every word.
4. **Listening:** Numbers, Prices & Phone Numbers (`curric_a1_listen_numbers`)
   - Strategy: Distinguishing phonetic minimal pairs (`-teen` vs `-ty`).
5. **Writing:** Basic Sentence Structure (SVO) (`curric_a1_write_sentences`)
   - Mechanics: Subject + Verb + Object, capital letters, terminal periods.
6. **Speaking:** Self-Introductions & Personal Information (`curric_a1_speak_introductions`)
   - Structure: Name $\rightarrow$ Origin $\rightarrow$ Occupation.
7. **Pronunciation:** Alphabet, Vowels & Basic Consonants (`curric_a1_pron_alphabet`)
   - Phonetics: Contrastive vowel lengths (/iː/ vs /ɪ/, /uː/ vs /ʊ/).
8. **Functional English:** Greetings, Politeness & Directions (`curric_a1_func_greetings`)
   - Pragmatics: Formal vs informal greetings; `Please`, `Thank you`, `Excuse me`.
9. **Review & Assessment:** A1 Milestone Evaluation (`curric_a1_review_milestone`)

#### Mastery & Remediation Criteria
- **Pass Threshold:** $\ge 60\%$ on guided drills; $\ge 70\%$ on milestone quiz.
- **Remediation Trigger:** Inability to inflect third-person singular `-s` re-routes learner to Present Simple concord drills.

---

### Level A2: Elementary (IELTS Equivalent: Band 3.5–4.0)

#### Learning Outcomes
- Communicate in simple, routine tasks requiring a direct exchange of information on familiar topics.
- Describe in simple terms aspects of past background, immediate environment, and immediate needs.
- Write simple descriptive personal letters, short emails, and chronological past narratives.

#### Module Progression
1. **Grammar:** Past Simple & Regular/Irregular Verbs (`curric_a2_gram_past_simple`)
   - Affirmative ($S + V_{ed} / V_2$), Negative ($S + \text{did not} + V_{base}$), Interrogative ($\text{Did} + S + V_{base}?$).
2. **Vocabulary:** Travel, Food, Dining & Directions (`curric_a2_vocab_travel_food`)
   - Countable vs uncountable nouns; quantifiers (`some`, `any`, `much`, `many`, `a lot of`).
3. **Reading:** Scanning Menus, Advertisements & Itineraries (`curric_a2_read_menus`)
   - Strategy: High-speed target word scanning; calculating costs and times.
4. **Listening:** Following Spoken Directions & Announcements (`curric_a2_listen_directions`)
   - Strategy: Spatial prepositions (`opposite`, `next to`, `turn left at`).
5. **Writing:** Informal Letters & Descriptive Emails (`curric_a2_write_emails`)
   - Conventions: Salutations, paragraphing with coordinating conjunctions (`and`, `but`, `so`, `because`).
6. **Speaking:** Describing Past Experiences & Holidays (`curric_a2_speak_past_experiences`)
   - Discourse: Chronological sequence markers (`First`, `After that`, `Finally`).
7. **Pronunciation:** Regular Past Tense Endings (`/t/`, `/d/`, `/ɪd/`) (`curric_a2_pron_past_endings`)
   - Phonetics: Voiced vs unvoiced consonants conditioning `-ed` inflection.
8. **Functional English:** Making Purchases & Transactional Dialogue (`curric_a2_func_shopping`)
   - Pragmatics: Asking prices, asking sizes, requesting refunds politely.
9. **Review & Assessment:** A2 Milestone Evaluation (`curric_a2_review_milestone`)

#### Mastery & Remediation Criteria
- **Pass Threshold:** $\ge 65\%$ on guided drills; $\ge 70\%$ on milestone quiz.
- **Remediation Trigger:** Errors with `did not + past verb` (e.g. *"didn't went"*) triggers immediate auxiliary base-verb remediation.

---

### Level B1: Intermediate (IELTS Equivalent: Band 4.5–5.0)

#### Learning Outcomes
- Maintain interaction and express what is wanted in a range of contexts (travel, work, hobbies).
- Describe experiences, events, dreams, hopes, and ambitions, and briefly give reasons and explanations for opinions.
- Construct coherent paragraphs with explicit topic sentences and supporting examples.

#### Module Progression
1. **Grammar:** Present Perfect vs Past Simple & Aspect (`curric_b1_gram_present_perfect`)
   - Form & Meaning: Indefinite past experience vs definite completed past action; time markers (`since`, `for`, `already`, `yet`, `just`).
2. **Vocabulary:** Workplace Dynamics, Careers & Higher Education (`curric_b1_vocab_workplace_education`)
   - Word families: `employ` $\rightarrow$ `employer` $\rightarrow$ `employee` $\rightarrow$ `employment`.
3. **Reading:** Skimming for Gist & Scanning for Details (`curric_b1_read_skimming`)
   - Strategy: Heading matching and distinguishing main themes from minor illustrations.
4. **Listening:** Note-Taking from Short Academic Lectures (`curric_b1_listen_lectures`)
   - Strategy: Identifying lecture discourse markers and utilizing shorthand symbols.
5. **Writing:** Paragraph Architecture & Topic Sentences (`curric_b1_write_paragraphs`)
   - Structure: Controlling idea + Supporting sentences + Concrete evidence + Concluding summary.
6. **Speaking:** Expressing Opinions & Justifying Viewpoints (`curric_b1_speak_opinions_justification`)
   - Discourse: Formulaic opinion markers (`In my view`, `From my perspective`, `The primary reason is`).
7. **Pronunciation:** Word Stress Patterns & The Schwa (`/ə/`) Sound (`curric_b1_pron_word_stress`)
   - Phonetics: Noun-verb stress shifts (`'record` vs `re'cord`); identifying unstressed vowel reduction.
8. **Functional English:** Professional Emails & Formal Requests (`curric_b1_func_business_emails`)
   - Pragmatics: Politeness softening (`I was wondering if you could...` vs `I want...`).
9. **Review & Assessment:** B1 Milestone Evaluation (`curric_b1_review_milestone`)

#### Mastery & Remediation Criteria
- **Pass Threshold:** $\ge 70\%$ on guided drills; $\ge 75\%$ on milestone quiz.
- **Remediation Trigger:** Conflating `since` and `for` or using Present Perfect with specific past time indicators (e.g. *"I have seen him yesterday"*) triggers aspectual drill remediation.

---

### Level B2: Upper Intermediate (IELTS Equivalent: Band 5.5–6.5)

#### Learning Outcomes
- Present and defend opinions in discussions, conduct sustained argumentation, evaluate alternative proposals.
- Comprehend the main ideas of propositionally and linguistically complex discourse on concrete and abstract topics.
- Author clear, well-structured essays (PEEL structure) with appropriate cohesive devices and register.

#### Module Progression
1. **Grammar:** Conditionals: Real, Unreal & Mixed (`curric_b2_gram_conditionals`)
   - Forms: Second ($If + \text{Past Simple}, \text{would} + V$), Third ($If + \text{Past Perfect}, \text{would have} + V_3$), and Mixed Conditionals.
2. **Vocabulary:** Technology, AI & Environmental Science (`curric_b2_vocab_technology_environment`)
   - Academic Lexis: Collocations with `sustainability`, `mitigation`, `algorithmic`, `biodiversity`.
3. **Reading:** Identifying Writer Views & Claims (Y/N/NG) (`curric_b2_read_claims`)
   - Strategy: Distinguishing factual refutation (FALSE/NO) from absence of verification (NOT GIVEN).
4. **Listening:** Following Academic Debates & Conflicting Views (`curric_b2_listen_debates`)
   - Strategy: Tracking multiple speakers, concessions (`Granted, but...`), and implicit qualifiers.
5. **Writing:** PEEL Paragraphs & Cohesive Linking (`curric_b2_write_peel`)
   - Mechanics: Point $\rightarrow$ Explanation $\rightarrow$ Evidence $\rightarrow$ Link; complex transitional adverbs (`Nevertheless`, `Consequently`).
6. **Speaking:** Complex Arguments & Part 3 Discussion (`curric_b2_speak_complex_arguments`)
   - Strategy: 3-step speaking method (Direct Stance $\rightarrow$ Analytical Justification $\rightarrow$ Concrete Example).
7. **Pronunciation:** Connected Speech, Linking & Elision (`curric_b2_pron_connected_speech`)
   - Phonetics: Catenation (consonant-to-vowel), intrusive `/r/`, `/j/`, `/w/`, and consonant cluster reduction.
8. **Functional English:** Leading Meetings & Diplomatic Disagreement (`curric_b2_func_meetings`)
   - Pragmatics: Tactful hedging (`I take your point, however...`, `Could we consider...`).
9. **Review & Assessment:** B2 Milestone Evaluation (`curric_b2_review_milestone`)

#### Mastery & Remediation Criteria
- **Pass Threshold:** $\ge 70\%$ on guided drills; $\ge 80\%$ on milestone quiz.
- **Remediation Trigger:** Overuse of informal conversational transitionals (e.g. *"like"*, *"and besides"*) in formal writing triggers register re-training.

---

### Level C1: Advanced (IELTS Equivalent: Band 7.0–8.0)

#### Learning Outcomes
- Understand a wide range of demanding, longer texts and recognize implicit sociocultural meanings.
- Express ideas fluently, spontaneously, and flexibly for social, academic, and professional purposes.
- Produce clear, smoothly flowing, complex texts showing controlled use of organizational patterns, connectors, and cohesive devices.

#### Module Progression
1. **Grammar:** Inversion, Cleft Sentences & Fronting (`curric_c1_gram_inversion_cleft`)
   - Forms: Negative inversion (`Seldom has...`, `Not only did...`), wh-clefts (`What concerns researchers is...`), it-clefts (`It was the policy that...`).
2. **Vocabulary:** Academic Lexis, Nominalization & Precision (`curric_c1_vocab_academic_lexis`)
   - Transformation: Converting verbal clauses into compact nominalized academic syntax.
3. **Reading:** Evaluating Argument Strength, Bias & Tone (`curric_c1_read_evaluating_arguments`)
   - Critical Analysis: Deconstructing rhetorical fallacies, ideological bias, and authorial stance.
4. **Listening:** Synthesizing Information Across Multiple Speakers (`curric_c1_listen_synthesis`)
   - Advanced Comprehension: Reconstructing implicit premises and contrasting academic methodologies.
5. **Writing:** Counter-Arguments, Rebuttal & Nuanced Thesis (`curric_c1_write_counterarguments`)
   - Advanced Structure: Concession $\rightarrow$ Methodological Critique $\rightarrow$ Rebuttal $\rightarrow$ Qualified Thesis.
6. **Speaking:** Nuance, Hedging & Speculative Discourse (`curric_c1_speak_nuance_hedging`)
   - Epistemic Modality: Modulating assertions (`It is conceivable that...`, `The evidence tentatively indicates...`).
7. **Pronunciation:** Contrastive Intonation & Tonic Prominence (`curric_c1_pron_intonation`)
   - Phonology: Nucleus placement altering semantic focus; fall-rise contours signaling reservation.
8. **Functional English:** Negotiation, Bargaining & Conflict Resolution (`curric_c1_func_negotiation`)
   - Strategic Discourse: Conditional concessions, framing non-negotiables diplomatically.
9. **Review & Assessment:** C1 Milestone Evaluation (`curric_c1_review_milestone`)

#### Mastery & Remediation Criteria
- **Pass Threshold:** $\ge 75\%$ on guided drills; $\ge 80\%$ on milestone quiz.
- **Remediation Trigger:** Failure to apply subject-auxiliary inversion after negative fronted adverbials triggers syntactic inversion drills.

---

### Level C2: Proficiency (IELTS Equivalent: Band 8.5–9.0)

#### Learning Outcomes
- Convey finer shades of meaning precisely using a wide range of qualifying devices.
- Command a broad lexical repertoire including idiomatic expressions and colloquialisms with awareness of connotative levels.
- Reconstruct arguments from different sources into a coherent presentation with stylistic elegance.

#### Module Progression
1. **Grammar:** The Subjunctive, Ellipsis & Stylistic Inversion (`curric_c2_gram_subjunctive_ellipses`)
   - Forms: Mandative subjunctive (`demand that he be present`), gapping, pseudoclefts, stylistic preposing.
2. **Vocabulary:** Idiomatic Mastery, Metaphor & Rare Lexis (`curric_c2_vocab_idiomatic_mastery`)
   - Semantic Nuance: Subtle distinctions between literary and archaic synonyms; conceptual metaphors.
3. **Reading:** Dissecting Irony, Subtext & Dense Philosophy (`curric_c2_read_subtext`)
   - Hermeneutics: Decoding satire, litotes, implicit irony, and multi-layered philosophical argumentation.
4. **Listening:** Rapid Colloquial Speech, Jargon & Obscure Accents (`curric_c2_listen_rapid_speech`)
   - Acoustic Agility: Deciphering high-speed dialectal elisions, stream-of-consciousness, and technical idioms.
5. **Writing:** Master Stylistics, Rhetorical Devices & Publication Prose (`curric_c2_write_mastery`)
   - Rhetoric: Chiasmus, anaphora, periodic sentences, balanced cadences for publication-grade essays.
6. **Speaking:** Spontaneous Discourse & Native-Like Idiom (`curric_c2_speak_spontaneous_discourse`)
   - Flow: Effortless conversational turns, witty rejoinders, effortless socio-pragmatic register switching.
7. **Pronunciation:** Voice Modulation, Dramatic Pausing & Rhetorical Impact (`curric_c2_pron_modulation`)
   - Suprasegmentals: Controlled tempo changes, theatrical pauses, persuasive oratorical resonance.
8. **Functional English:** High-Stakes Public Speaking & Keynote Delivery (`curric_c2_func_keynote`)
   - Public Oratory: Commanding auditoriums, field hostile interrogations, rhetorical persuasion.
9. **Review & Assessment:** C2 Master Capstone Examination (`curric_c2_review_milestone`)

#### Mastery & Remediation Criteria
- **Pass Threshold:** $\ge 80\%$ on guided drills; $\ge 85\%$ on milestone quiz.
- **Remediation Trigger:** Inaccurate subjunctive inflection (e.g. *"insisted that he goes"*) triggers subjunctive mood remediation.

## 4. Remediation Pathways & Mistake-Book Coupling

Every module is tightly coupled to the learner's `MistakeBookRepository`:
- **Categorization:** Errors logged during assessments are tagged with error taxonomy:
  - `grammar_tense_aspect`
  - `grammar_concord`
  - `grammar_inversion`
  - `vocabulary_collocation`
  - `vocabulary_preposition`
  - `pronunciation_stress`
  - `discourse_cohesion`
- **Dynamic Routing:** When a learner's error recurrence count in a specific taxonomy reaches $\ge 2$, `CurriculumEngine.recommend_review_lessons()` automatically prioritizes the corresponding foundational lesson in the learner's active study plan.
